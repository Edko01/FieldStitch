    Process

    Import photos, then choose the processing options.

    1. Find features
    2. Match overlapping photos
    3. Align cameras
    4. Build surface
    5. Render orthomosaic

    Orthomosaic

    Layers

    • Aerial imagery from the USDA, usually 1–3 years old. Needs internet the first time an area is shown; it is then kept with the project.

    Areas

    Areas become available once there are photos or an orthomosaic.

    Planning the mapping flight
    1. Draw the field on the satellite map (Polygon), or import its boundary.
    2. Send it to Pilot 2 with the button next to the field.
    3. After the flight, and process them.

    Split the orthomosaic into fields or tiles and export each one as its own GeoTIFF. Mark obstacles (trees, poles, buildings) inside a field for spray drones.

    Drag a corner to move it. Drag or click a small middle dot to add a corner. Right-click a corner, or click it and press Delete, to remove it. Ctrl+Z undoes.
